About AHMAD CHEW

AHMAD CHEW is a dedicated web-based examination prep platform designed specifically for Community Health Extension Worker (CHEW) students across Nigeria.

The question bank and study materials are constructed in alignment with the Nigerian CHEW curriculum, Community Health principles, and standard Primary Health Care Standing Orders.
